a picture you can open.每一頁的最後，

Every subpage ends with the same painting. Seven things in it each open a page of the wiki: the field opens Plants, the farmer opens Integrated Human Practices, the screen on the reactor opens Digital Twin.每個子頁的最後都放同一幅畫。畫裡七樣東西各連到 wiki 的一頁：點農田去 Plants，點農夫去 Integrated Human Practices，點反應器上的螢幕去 Digital Twin。

The design: B, the field edge設計：B 田邊近景

Painted in the manner of the homepage's vision painting. Nothing moves and nothing covers the picture: the thing under the pointer is traced in white, a small name tag sits above it, and its line of text appears in the caption underneath. The first time the picture comes into view, each thing catches the light once, left to right, so the reader sees what can be opened without being told.照首頁 vision 那幅畫的畫法。東西不會動，也沒有東西蓋住畫面：指到的東西被一圈白光描出輪廓，上方出現一個小小的名牌，說明文字出現在畫下方的那一行。畫第一次捲進畫面時，裡面的東西會由左到右各亮一下，讀者不用看說明就知道哪些可以點。

In a subpage放進子頁的樣子

The same few lines go on every subpage. The picture takes the place of Previous / Next, which it removes from the page itself. It works out which page the reader is on and marks that thing "You are here". Pick a page to see it.每個子頁放同一段程式。它取代原本的 Previous / Next，會自己把那一列拿掉。它也會認出讀者在哪一頁，把那樣東西標成「You are here」。點下面的頁面看看。

Brief for the illustrator給畫者的說明

The paintings on this page are placeholders drawn by code to settle the layout. The final art will be drawn by hand. What the drawing needs, so it drops straight into the page:這頁上的畫是程式畫的佔位圖，用來決定構圖。最後會請人手繪。為了讓畫好的圖可以直接放進網頁，需要符合這些條件：

  1. Same composition同樣的構圖Keep where things stand in B (and in the whole-wiki picture, if we use it). Changing it is fine, but tell us.東西的位置照 B（以及整個 wiki 那幅，如果要用的話）。要改也可以，請先說。
  2. One layer per thing每樣東西一個圖層The background on its own, then each clickable thing on its own layer with a transparent background: the field, the farmer, the student, the notebook, the reactor, its screen, its label.背景單獨一層，每樣可以點的東西各自一層、透明背景：農田、農夫、學生、筆記本、反應器、螢幕、警告標誌。
  3. Size尺寸2400 × 1000 px for B (2400 × 1200 for the whole wiki), PNG, all layers on the same canvas so they line up.B 是 2400 × 1000 px（整個 wiki 是 2400 × 1200），PNG，所有圖層用同一張畫布，位置才對得上。
  4. Style畫風Like the homepage's vision painting: flat colour in two or three tones, soft dry-brush edges, no black outlines, slightly cartoon.像首頁 vision 那幅畫：兩三個色調的平塗、柔和的乾刷邊、沒有黑框、帶一點卡通感。
  5. Must read at a glance一眼看得出來The ReLEAF shirt under the lab coat, the notebook in the student's hand, the screen on top of the reactor and the yellow warning label on its front.實驗衣下面的 ReLEAF T 恤、學生手上的筆記本、反應器頂上的螢幕、正面的黃色警告標誌。
  6. No real people, no words不畫真人，不寫字Generic figures only. Leave words out of the picture (the page writes the names), except "ReLEAF" on the shirt.只畫一般的人物。畫裡不要寫字（頁名由網頁顯示），T 恤上的「ReLEAF」除外。

Left to decide還要決定的事

  1. Seven pages, or the whole wiki?七頁，還是整個 wiki？The foot of every subpage could carry B (seven pages) or the whole-wiki picture (every page). Or B at the foot of subpages and the whole-wiki picture on the homepage.子頁底部可以放 B（七頁），也可以放整個 wiki 那幅（所有頁）。或是子頁放 B，首頁放整個 wiki 那幅。
  2. Are the objects right?東西選得對嗎？In the whole-wiki picture, is a chalkboard right for Math Model, a crate for Contribution, a signpost for Development? Say what fits your page better.整個 wiki 那幅裡，Math Model 用黑板、Contribution 用木箱、Development 用路標，對嗎？覺得有更適合你那頁的東西就說。
  3. The lines under the picture畫下方的說明Each page owner rewrites their line (one or two sentences).各頁負責人改寫自己那一行（一到兩句）。
  4. Who draws it誰來畫Send the illustrator the brief above.把上面的說明交給畫者。
