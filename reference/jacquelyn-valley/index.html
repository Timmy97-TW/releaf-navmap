The whole wiki in one valley

This is how the end of a ReLEAF subpage would look. Every page is a thing you can recognize. Things you can click have a white sticker edge, and a name appears when you point at one. The mascot stands next to the page you are on, and if you rest on something else he moonwalks over to it. Click a thing to open that page on the live wiki.

Swap pages to watch him walk there.

What is still a placeholder

  • This sample is clickable: click a sticker and the picture turns into that page's scene, the way it would when a reader follows the link on the real wiki.
  • Every page has its own small scene with the page itself in (or close to) the middle and 5 to 6 related pages around it, all clickable stickers.
  • Things are grouped by the story along one path: lab things inside a small open lab station, the team and project things, the farm (the farmer always on a crop field, the bioreactor always beside it), and the village.
  • Where the link table had fewer than five links, related pages were added: Results, Experiments, Parts, Plants, Measurement, Safety, Notebook, Math Model, Hardware, Digital Twin, Protein Design, Human Practices, Education, Entrepreneurship, Data Physicalization, Members and Milestones.
  • The art is still the code-drawn placeholder from the navmap repository, repainted for the new layout. Hand-drawn art can replace it object by object, at the same sizes and positions.
  • The mascot is cut out of the homepage's moonwalk animation, so a few white edges show around his hat. A transparent drawing of him would fix that.
  • The name tag uses shorter names than the menu for four pages: Human Practices, Laws & Regulations, Geospatial and Team Members.
  • On a phone the valley keeps a readable size and scrolls sideways, starting where you are.
